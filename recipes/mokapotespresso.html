How to prepare fine cup of espresso

  1. Boil Water
  2. Fill bottom champer with water up to valve
  3. Put the basket on top and fill with coffee
  4. carefully put the top champer
  5. put the moka on stovetop on low to medium heat and wait for coffee to come up
  6. when you see the bubbles turn off the heat and cool the moke under cold water then pour the coffee to your favourite cup
  7. Enjoy your drink :D
